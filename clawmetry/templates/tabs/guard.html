Policies

A policy can act automatically when a detector reports a condition. New policies start in monitor mode. They record the proposed action without changing the agent. An enabled policy can escalate its actions over time. For example, it can pause an agent, then kill it five minutes later if the condition continues. Each step requires the condition to remain active when the waiting period ends.
