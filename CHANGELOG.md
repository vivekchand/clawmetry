### Release: every number on your dashboard now says how it was worked out (carries #5215) (2026-08-26)
- **Who this reaches:** everyone. Every dollar amount and every score on the Cost tab, the Overview spend card and the Guard list now carries a small label saying whether it was measured, worked out from measurements, or estimated, with the sum on hover.
- **The four words, and what they mean.** *Measured* means we read it off a record your agent wrote. *Derived* means we worked it out from measurements by a rule you can check, which is where almost all cost figures land: your tools rarely write down a price, so we count the tokens and price them against the published rate card. *Estimated* means there is an assumption inside it that can be wrong. And *no basis* means we could not work it out at all, and we say so instead of showing you a number.
- **Nothing is shown where nothing is known.** This is the part worth having. Hover any figure and you get the sum that produced it, the exact period it covers, the numbers that went into it, and where those came from. You can check our arithmetic. Most of this industry asks you to take the figure on faith, and at least one competitor's own documentation warns you not to quote its dollars to the cent while its dashboard shows exactly that.
- **A zero you earned and a zero we invented no longer look alike.** A failed read of the local database once went out as "$0.00" and was read, correctly, as a real result. It cannot happen now: label a figure as unknown and the number is removed, so nothing downstream can print a zero for it. A genuinely quiet day still shows $0.00, badged as measured, because that zero is true and hiding it would be its own kind of lie.
- **Three more places were doing the same thing, and are fixed.** On the free plan, history older than a day was being shown as twelve days on which you spent nothing; those days are now visibly held back rather than painted as zeroes. The spending card on the front page opened every load claiming "$0.00" before it had loaded anything, over a real four-figure month. And a stuck session nobody could put a price on was being ranked as a free one at the bottom of a list sorted by money.
- **The forecast admits it is a forecast.** "Projected month-end" was the most confident-looking number on the page and it assumes the rest of the month looks like the last seven days, which is wrong in exactly the quiet week you would quote it in. It is labelled as an estimate, and so is the projected saving from routing work to a cheaper model, which is a comparison against something that never happened.
- **Verified:** 43 new tests. One of them walks each real response, finds every money and score field by its shape rather than from a list somebody has to remember to update, and fails if any of them has no basis behind it. Another counts the places still unlabelled and refuses to let that number grow. The badges were then read off a real dashboard against a real machine's data, which is how we caught the weekly figure claiming it covered today.
